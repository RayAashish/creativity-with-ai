## 🔬 Interactive Simulation Modules
- **Physics**:
  - Cavendish Torsion Balance ($G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$) with laser deflection telemetry
  - 9-Planet Weight & Gravitational Field Comparison
  - Guinea-and-Feather Vacuum Free-Fall Drop
  - Pascal's Law Hydraulic Car Lift with $50\times$ mechanical advantage
  - Archimedes Upthrust & Law of Floatation Immersion Tank
  - Anomalous Expansion of Water ($0^\circ\text{C}$ to $15^\circ\text{C}$, maximum density at $4^\circ\text{C}$)
  - Convex Lens Ray Tracer & Human Eye Defect Correction (Myopia & Hypermetropia)
  - Step-Up/Down Transformer Simulator & NEA Domestic Electricity Bill Calculator
- **Chemistry**:
  - Laboratory preparation of Carbon Dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$) via Woulfe's bottle
  - Laboratory preparation of Ammonia ($\text{NH}_3$) with CaO lime drying tower
  - Interactive Bohr electron shell generator for all 118 periodic elements
- **Biology & Earth Science**:
  - Mendel's Monohybrid Cross Punnett Square ($3:1$ and $1:2:1$)
  - Human Sex Determination ($44+\text{XX}$ vs $44+\text{XY}$)
  - 4-Chambered Heart Double Circulation with pulsating blood pressure
  - Stellar Lifecycle progression & Himalayan Glacial Lake Outburst Flood (GLOF) dynamics
