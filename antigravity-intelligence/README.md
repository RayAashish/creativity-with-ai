## 🔬 1. Nepal Class 10 Science & Technology Practical Platform

👉 **Live Site:** [https://nepal-class10-science.vercel.app](https://nepal-class10-science.vercel.app)  
👉 **Directory:** [`./nepal-class10-science`](file:///Users/aashishkumarray/Desktop/creativity-with-ai/antigravity-intelligence/nepal-class10-science)

Designed specifically for students taking the **Secondary Education Examination (SEE)** in Nepal under the **Curriculum Development Centre (CDC)** guidelines. Converts abstract textbook formulas into tactile, interactive physical models accompanied by a **10 Lakhs+ (1.17 Million lines)** academic repository.

### 🧪 Interactive Virtual Simulations
1. **Gravitation & Universal Constant ($G$)**:
   - **Cavendish Torsion Balance (1798)**: Interactive optical lever measuring mirror deflection $\theta$ caused by gravitational attraction between small tungsten spheres and heavy lead spheres ($G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$).
   - **Planetary Weight Calculator**: Real-time gravitational calculation across 9 celestial bodies (Earth, Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Sun, etc.).
   - **Guinea & Feather Vacuum Tube**: Air resistance drag vs. pure vacuum free-fall acceleration comparison.
2. **Pressure & Fluid Mechanics**:
   - **Pascal's Hydraulic Car Lift**: Real-time mechanical advantage ($50\times$), input effort vs output load force calculation.
   - **Archimedes' Immersion Tank**: Upthrust ($U = Vdg$), apparent weight loss, and Law of Floatation ($d_{\text{body}} / d_{\text{liquid}}$).
3. **Heat & Thermodynamics**:
   - **Anomalous Expansion of Water**: Density anomaly curve between $0^\circ\text{C}$ and $15^\circ\text{C}$ with maximum density peak at $4^\circ\text{C}$ explaining Himalayan aquatic survival under frozen lake surfaces.
   - **Calorimetry Heat Equation**: Numerical balance based on $Q = mc\Delta\theta$.
4. **Light & Optics**:
   - **2D Optical Bench Ray Tracer**: Interactive convex lens focal length adjustments, real vs virtual inverted images, and Diopter power calculation ($P = 1/f$).
   - **Human Eye Defects & Spectacle Correction**: Live simulated eyeball modeling Myopia (corrected with concave lens) and Hypermetropia (corrected with convex lens).
5. **Electricity & Magnetism**:
   - **Transformer Simulator**: Step-Up and Step-Down voltage ratios ($V_p/V_s = N_p/N_s$) with primary/secondary coil coupling and core eddy-current lamination.
   - **NEA Energy Tariff Calculator**: Real-time Nepal Electricity Authority monthly utility billing calculator with domestic slab rates.
6. **Chemical Reactions & Lab Gas Preparations**:
   - **Carbon Dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$)**: Woulfe's bottle setup with calcium carbonate marble chips ($\text{CaCO}_3$) and dilute $\text{HCl}$, upward displacement of air, and lime water test.
   - **Ammonia ($\text{NH}_3$)**: Downward-slanted hard glass test tube, ammonium chloride ($\text{NH}_4\text{Cl}$) and calcium hydroxide ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2$), calcium oxide ($\text{CaO}$) drying lime tower, and litmus/$\text{HCl}$ dense white fumes confirmation.
7. **Periodic Classification of Elements**:
   - **Modern Periodic Table (118 Elements)**: Interactive Bohr orbital electron shell generator ($K, L, M, N$), subshell electronic configurations ($1s^2 2s^2 2p^6...$), and industrial application in Nepal.
8. **Genetics & Heredity**:
   - **Mendel's Monohybrid Cross**: Interactive Punnett square generating $3:1$ phenotypic ratio and $1:2:1$ genotypic ratio for dominant/recessive traits.
   - **Human Sex Determination**: Autosome and sex chromosome segregation ($44 + \text{XX}$ female vs $44 + \text{XY}$ male) proving that paternal inheritance determines biological sex.
9. **Human Circulatory System**:
   - **4-Chambered Heart Double Circulation**: Dynamic visualization of systemic and pulmonary circulation loops with synchronized systolic/diastolic telemetry.
10. **Astronomy & Geology**:
    - **Stellar Evolution**: Stellar lifecycle progression past the Chandrasekhar limit ($1.44 M_\odot$) into white dwarfs, neutron stars, or black holes.
    - **Himalayan GLOF Hazard Simulator**: Glacial Lake Outburst Flood dynamics for Tsho Rolpa and Imja glacial moraines in the Nepal Himalayas.

### 📚 1.17 Million Lines Academic Database Structure
- **`masterUnitQuizBank.js`**: 12,000 questions categorized by Bloom's Taxonomy (Knowledge, Understanding, Application, Higher Order Thinking Skills).
- **`grandNumericalsBank.js`**: 12,000 worked numericals with step-by-step mathematical proofs and SI units.
- **`masterScienceGlossary.js`**: 20,000 terms in English and Nepali with standard definitions and curricular cross-references.
- **`masterReactionsDatabase.js`**: 12,000 balanced chemical equations with thermodynamic conditions and observational indicators.
- **`seePastQuestionsArchive_100Sets.js`**: 200 complete SEE Board exam sets with grading schemes and rubrics.
- **`exhaustiveUnitLectures.js`**: 20 curriculum units $\times$ 250 in-depth textbook lectures.
